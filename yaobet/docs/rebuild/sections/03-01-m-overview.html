Global motion contract (FACT, src/styles/motion.css): under @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) all --dur-* become 0ms and *, *::before, *::after get animation-duration: 0.001ms !important; animation-iteration-count: 1 !important; transition-duration: 0.001ms !important; scroll-behavior: auto !important;. Every transition in this track therefore collapses to instant under reduced motion even where the component does not opt out itself.

Auth model used by every member surface

  • Source: src/lib/auth/session.tsx (MockAuthProvider, useAuth), src/lib/auth/guard.ts (useRequireAuth), src/lib/auth/fixtures.ts.
  • Initial state: src/app/providers.tsx mounts <MockAuthProvider initialState="tiered">, so the app boots LOGGED IN as the tiered user. Three states: guest | level-0 | tiered. isAuthenticated = state !== "guest".
  • useRequireAuth() returns { authState, allowed: isAuthenticated, promptAuth(resumeIntent?) }. It NEVER redirects; promptAuth calls openAuthModal({ mode: "login", resumeIntent }) (auth modal = track S). ResumeIntent = { action: "deposit" | "withdraw" | "none"; isFirstDeposit?: boolean }.
  • Logout: signOut() sets state to guest (pure client state; no navigation).
