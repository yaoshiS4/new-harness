ScratchCardGame / ScratchCardStage (the ticket overlay)

1. Purpose

A modal scratch ticket: scratch the gold coating off four winning numbers and twelve of your numbers; when both halves are open the result appears.

2. Source

/* ============================================================================
 * ScratchCardGame — the ticket: a deep warm panel carrying two coated
 * sections, published odds and the dev fence.
 *
 * Panel width (560px desktop dialog) comes from Overlay `desktopMaxWidth`.
 * Semantic / --brand-* indirection vars only — never a raw ramp literal
 * (--gold-*, --cta*, --font-body/-num, --text-on-gold). token-lint A2.
 *
 * ON THE GRADIENTS. The flat ban is about CARD and BUTTON fills; it is why
 * every surface here is still a flat token and why there is not one box-shadow
 * in the file — the gold hairline carries every edge, as everywhere else. The
 * radial gradients below are LARGE DECORATION on a game surface: the warm bloom

10. Behaviour & a11y

  • First card dealt in a mount effect (never during render). Body remounts per open, so closing resets everything.
  • Overlay: role dialog/aria-modal, focus trap, Esc and scrim close, return focus to "Cào ngay", body scroll lock; swipe-to-dismiss disabled so downward strokes don't close the sheet.
  • Value lists are aria-hidden until their section is open; the sr-only sentence per cell then exposes number, match and prize.
  • "Mở tất cả" is the keyboard/screen-reader path; the canvas is aria-hidden and unfocusable.
shortVnd's comment says the full figure is printed "from 480px up"; the CSS switch is at 560px. The breakpoints are viewport-based while the ticket lives in a ≤560px dialog, so at ≥560px viewport each cell (~100px) must fit "20.000.000 ₫" at 12px (inference: fits, tightly). The dev bar ("Buộc kết quả") ships in the player-facing overlay.
