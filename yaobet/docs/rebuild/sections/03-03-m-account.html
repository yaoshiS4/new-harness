Behaviour: initial state "ho-so" (SSR-safe), then useEffect reads window.location.hash and subscribes to hashchange. Consequence: on a hard load of /tai-khoan#bao-mat the first paint shows ProfileBasic, then swaps to SecurityCenter after hydration (flash). No transition/animation on swap. Panels are fully unmounted on switch (form state is lost).
